# Mena Karam

Research portfolio in robotic perception, 3D computer vision, geometric reconstruction, and robot control.

This website presents selected academic and technical work spanning physically grounded image restoration, 3D reconstruction from visual data, robotic locomotion and control, embedded multi-sensor systems, and ROS-based manipulation. Across these projects, the central theme is how sensing quality, geometry, and control interact in real robotic systems and research-oriented engineering workflows.

## Research focus

Current research interests lie at the intersection of physically grounded perception, geometric modelling, and embodied robotics. Particular emphasis is placed on hybrid perception systems in which explicit geometric, photometric, and dynamical models provide an interpretable structural foundation, while learning-based components address variability, uncertainty, and phenomena that cannot be specified reliably from first principles.

- Physically grounded perception and image formation
- 3D computer vision, geometric reconstruction and scene understanding
- Mapping, spatial representation, and uncertainty-aware estimation for robotics
- Hybrid model-based and learning-based perception methods
- Locomotion, manipulation, and embodied robot control
- Robust perception and decision pipelines for real-world robotic systems

## Selected projects

### Coupled Deblurring and 3D Reconstruction from Image Sequences
A Master’s thesis on microscopic image sequences acquired during lunar rover-related ground testing, focused on modeling defocus blur and improving downstream 3D reconstruction quality through spatially varying image restoration and reconstruction-aware analysis.

### 3D Models of Planetary Surfaces in Virtual Reality
A reconstruction project based on NASA Perseverance rover imagery, with workflow based on stereo geometry, structure from motion, bundle adjustment, point-cloud registration, and geometric refinement to generate terrain models for spatial inspection in VR environment.

### Teen-Sized Humanoid Robot: Modeling and Control
A humanoid robotics project for Bachelor’s thesis involving forward and inverse kinematics, joint-trajectory generation, ZMP-based gait generation, state-space and model-predictive control, multibody simulation, and ROS/Gazebo-based integration.

### Omniwheel Robot: Embedded Perception and Closed-Loop Control
An omnidirectional wheeled robot project combining encoder, ultrasonic, infrared, magnetic, and camera sensing with PWM actuation, real-time control logic, and embedded communication.

### UR10 Motion Control in ROS
A ROS-based manipulator-control project involving inverse kinematics, trajectory generation, Gazebo simulation, RViz visualization, and action-based trajectory execution.
