# Methods and Tools

## Perception and imaging

Selected experience includes:

- Image formation, optical and depth from defocus modeling
- Physically grounded blur analysis
- Spatially varying image restoration
- Sharpness estimation and reconstruction-aware preprocessing
- Real-time vision foundations in robotics

## 3D computer vision and reconstruction

Methods used across projects include:

- Calibrated and uncalibrated stereo vision
- Structure from motion
- Multi-view stereo reconstruction
- Bundle adjustment
- Dense point-cloud generation
- Geometric comparison and refinement
- ICP- and RANSAC-based alignment

## Robotics, locomotion, and control

Relevant methods include:

- Forward and inverse kinematics
- Joint-trajectory generation
- ZMP-based gait planning
- State-space control
- Model-predictive control
- Closed-loop mobile-robot control
- Multi-sensor actuation-feedback integration

## Mapping, localisation, and robot navigation foundations

Relevant coursework and technical preparation include:

- Occupancy-grid and localisation concepts
- Monte Carlo localisation foundations
- Laser-based perception and scan processing
- Path planning and obstacle avoidance
- Timed Elastic Bands exposure through robotics coursework
